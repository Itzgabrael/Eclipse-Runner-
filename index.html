The Rules of Light

The world cycles between Light and Eclipse.

In Light, hidden platforms fade from view and enemies see you clearly, but move slower.

In Eclipse, some enemies go blind, but shadow spikes rise from the dark, and a Seeker hunts by sound alone — stay still, stay safe.

Your Light Pulse reveals hidden platforms and stuns nearby shadow enemies. It costs energy — recharge by grabbing Light Orbs or waiting.

Press against a wall in mid-air to wall-slide, then jump again to wall-jump off it.

Near the end, the Warden guards a spike-lined gauntlet — it never sleeps, in light or dark. Pulse-stun it, then dash past.

Collect ◆ Fragments and reach the beacon at the end of the run.
